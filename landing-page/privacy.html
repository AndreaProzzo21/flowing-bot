Privacy & Cookies Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FlowingBot Lab is built as a static website. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or any marketing tracking scripts. We do not profile visitors or sell data.

However, to ensure security and provide core functionalities, minimal technical data processing occurs as outlined below.

1. Server Logs (Nginx)

Like most web servers, our infrastructure automatically records basic request information. This data is strictly used for security monitoring, troubleshooting, and preventing abuse.

  • Data collected: IP address, browser type (User-Agent), requested pages, and timestamps.
  • Retention: Logs are rotated and permanently deleted after a standard retention period (usually 14 days), unless required for investigating a specific security incident.

2. Blog Interactions (Giscus)

Our blog comment section is powered by Giscus, which leverages the GitHub Discussions API. This allows us to provide an interactive space without maintaining a user database.

  • Reading articles: The Giscus script communicates with GitHub servers to load existing comments.
  • Posting comments: Authentication is handled directly through GitHub. FlowingBot Lab does not access or store your credentials. The content of your comment is stored publicly in our GitHub repository.
  • Cookies: Giscus and GitHub may utilize technical and session cookies required for authentication and state management. For details, refer to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Third-Party Fonts

To ensure proper text rendering, this website utilizes Google Fonts. When your browser requests these resources, your IP address is briefly visible to Google servers. For more information, please review the Google Fonts Privacy FAQ.

4. Contact Communications

If you reach out via our contact email (info@flowingbot.com), we will process your email address and message contents solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR), you have rights regarding your personal data, including access, correction, and deletion. Because we do not maintain user profiles, the only identifiable information we may hold is an IP address within temporary logs or an email thread.

For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at info@flowingbot.com.
